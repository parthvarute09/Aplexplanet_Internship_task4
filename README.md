# 📈 Project Workflow

### 1️⃣ Data Storytelling

- Reviewed findings from previous tasks
- Identified key business insights
- Organized insights into a logical narrative
- Highlighted business impact
- Suggested strategic recommendations

---

### 2️⃣ Business Presentation

Created a professional presentation including:

- Executive Summary
- Business Problem
- Data Analysis
- Visual Insights
- Key Findings
- Recommendations
- Conclusion

---

### 3️⃣ Hypothesis Testing

Example Business Hypothesis

**Null Hypothesis (H₀):**
There is no significant difference in sales after implementing the new strategy.

**Alternative Hypothesis (H₁):**
The new strategy significantly improves sales.

Statistical Test Used:

- Independent T-Test
- Confidence Level: 95%
- Significance Level (α): 0.05

---

### 4️⃣ Statistical Interpretation

- Calculated p-value
- Compared p-value with significance level
- Accepted or rejected the null hypothesis
- Explained the business implications
